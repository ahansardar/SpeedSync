# SpeedSync Discovery Notes

SpeedSync is easiest to understand as a browser-first GPS speedometer and
journey tracker.

## Who It Helps

- Cyclists, runners, walkers, riders, and travelers who want a quick speed view.
- Builders learning browser geolocation, maps, PWA behavior, and local storage.
- Developers looking for a compact Next.js example with a mobile-first interface.

## Why It Is Worth Starring

- It is usable from the live site without setup.
- It keeps journey data local instead of sending location history to a server.
- It combines speed tracking, transport modes, journey history, maps, alerts, and
  an achievement system in one project.
- The codebase is approachable for small UI, analytics, and PWA contributions.

## Good First Improvements

- Add fresh screenshots from the live app.
- Add install-to-home-screen instructions for mobile browsers.
- Add a short privacy section to the live app itself.
- Add test coverage around speed and distance calculations.
